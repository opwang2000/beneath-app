Support

Beneath is a free focus app for the Mac (macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon). It has no account, no network and no in-app purchases. It can be used entirely with VoiceOver, Voice Control or the keyboard.

Contact: email beneathsupport@gmail.com with any question, problem or suggestion. Every message gets a reply, usually within a few days. (If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue.)

Common questions

  • I closed the window. How do I get it back? Choose Window › Beneath in the menu bar, or click Beneath in the Dock. Closing the window doesn't quit Beneath; quit it with Beneath › Quit Beneath (⌘Q).
  • Where are my goals and texts kept? Only on your Mac. Goals are saved automatically; in Text Mode each text is a plain text file in the folder you chose (shown in Settings).
  • Why is the sky different from what I see outside? Beneath follows the real sun and moon for your place and time, while the weather is chosen in the Sky menu (or left random).

支持

Beneath 是一款免费的 Mac 专注应用（需要 macOS 15 及以上、Apple 芯片）。没有账号，不联网，没有任何内购，可以完全用旁白（VoiceOver）、语音控制或键盘操作。

联系方式：有任何问题、故障或建议，请发邮件到 beneathsupport@gmail.com，每封都会回复，通常几天内。（有 GitHub 账号的话，也可以在这里提交。）

常见问题

  • 关掉窗口后怎么找回来？在菜单栏选择窗口 › Beneath，或点程序坞里的 Beneath。关闭窗口不会退出 Beneath；要退出请选择 Beneath › 退出 Beneath（⌘Q）。
  • 我的目标和文本存在哪里？只存在你的 Mac 上。目标会自动保存；文本模式下，每篇文本都是你所选文件夹里的一个纯文本文件（在设置里可以看到）。
  • 为什么天空和窗外不一样？Beneath 按你所在地点和时间的真实太阳、月亮运行，天气则由"天空"菜单选择（或随机）。
