Support

Beneath is a free focus app for the Mac (macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon). It has no account, no network and no in-app purchases. It can be used entirely with VoiceOver, Voice Control or the keyboard.

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue and it will be answered there.

支持
